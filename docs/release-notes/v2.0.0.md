# Canopi 2.0.0

Canopi 2 makes the map the canvas. Every Design is drawn directly over a basemap, satellite imagery or your own terrain data, and every plant, zone, note and measurement is stored where it is on Earth. There is no separate drawing sheet and no site location to set: pan, zoom and search move only the view, never your objects. Desktop and Web share the same canvas, panels, stories and PDF export; Desktop adds the full Plant catalog, the Data library and the Design notebook.

## What's new

### Designing on the map

- A new Design opens with "Where is your site?": search a place name or type coordinates, and the map flies there. Fit to Design and Return to Design frame what the title bar, rails and open panel leave visible.
- Backgrounds: OpenFreeMap map styles (Liberty, Positron, Bright, Dark) with labels in your language, satellite imagery (free, or your own Google Maps key kept on this device), or None. Contour lines and hillshading from online elevation are one switch away.
- Every drawing tool shows a card beside the tool rail with what to do next and its keys. Place plants previews the plant under the pointer with its mature width and the distance to its nearest neighbour; Plant a row takes an interval; Place a stamp turns with [ and ].
- The floating selection toolbar is gone. Right-click an object (or press the Menu key) for one menu in plain words: Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Select all of this species, Plant color, Plant symbol, Show name, Arrange, Save as stamp, Lock, Rotate…, Species details, Add to calendar…, Set unit cost… and Rename zone….
- A chip at the bottom of the map names the selection ("12 plants · 3 species · 0.52 m apart", a zone's area and perimeter, a measurement's length). Zones can be named; an unnamed zone is called by its shape and size ("Rectangle zone · 120 m²").
- Rotate by a typed angle or in 15° steps, nudge with the arrow keys (10 cm, or 1 m with Shift), lock and unlock objects, and Unlock all in one step. Shift keeps polygon edges on 45° angles.
- Plant symbols were redrawn in one colour: 33 symbols in four families (plant form, what it gives, what it does, abstract marks), each taking any colour and staying legible at small sizes, on the map, in panels and in print.
- Plants in this Design › Display on the map colours plants by species, by stratum or in one colour, sets symbol size and outline, and shows labels as codes or names. Soften background dims busy imagery under your plants.
- Text and labels stay readable in the dark theme and over satellite: their colour follows the map, with a contrasting halo.

### Finding and placing plants

- The Plant catalog opens on recommended species (edible and multi-use first) and sorts by Name, Height or Edibility. Quick filters (Edible, Nitrogen fixer, Trees, Shrubs) sit under the search; other filters wait behind Filters and show as removable tokens.
- Species details lead with key facts: form, stratum, height, hardiness with USDA zones and lowest temperature, light, soil, moisture, growth and uses. Photos name their source and licence. A species already in your Design shows its code and count, with Select them and Zoom to them.
- Every plant list (Plants in this Design, Budget, Consortium, Calendar targets, Favorites) has the same finder (Ctrl F): common names in every language Canopi knows, scientific names and codes, ignoring accents, capitals and small typos. Selected on map, Stratum and Form narrow any list; matches are ringed on the map with Zoom to them and Select all.
- Place plants works before you pick a species: its card offers the species already in the Design, your Favorites, recent picks, a search and the full catalog.
- A species with no name in your language shows its English name marked "(en)", in lists, details and the PDF.

### Layers, data and analyses

- Layers is one list in three sections: Design (your layers), Site data (terrain and height data this Design shows, with results nested under their source) and Background.
- Add data imports single-band GeoTIFF terrain or height tiles (such as IGN LiDAR HD MNT, MNS and MNH). Before importing, Canopi says whether the files cover your site. Import runs while you keep working, with its progress and Cancel under Site data.
- The Data library keeps imported data on this computer and shares it between Designs. Remove from Design never deletes data; Delete everywhere does, and says so.
- Analyze… calculates Slope (degrees or percent) from ground elevation. Details show what a result was calculated from, whether it is out of date (with Refresh) and its processing history, and offer Run again with changes.
- Read values shows the value under the pointer; Fit to data frames the item.

### Planning panels

- Calendar: Range, One day or Unscheduled in one control, a Completed switch, notes on several lines, and a week that starts on your language's first day. Add to calendar… creates an action for the selected plants or a zone.
- Budget: Set unit cost… from the right-click menu opens the species' price; Selected on map narrows the list; export as CSV from File › Export.
- Consortium assigns each species a stratum (Emergent, High, Mid, Low) and succession phases; the map's stratum colours and the Stratum filter follow it.
- Budget, Calendar and Consortium show each species in the colour the map draws.

### Saved views and stories

- View › Save current view… keeps where the map looks, with its background, layers, labels and focused species, inside the Design. Saved views and Manage views… show a picture of each; going to one moves only the camera.
- The Stories panel (Ctrl 9) builds a story from views: add the current view as a step, give it a title, text with bold, italic, bullets and links, and pictures (up to 1 MB each, 10 MB per Design). Drag steps or use Alt ↑ and Alt ↓; duplicate, move or delete them with Undo.
- Present shows a story full-window on desktop, web and phones: the step's text and pictures beside the live map, step dots, Previous and Next, arrow keys, Space and Esc, and swiping on a phone. Presenting never changes the Design.

### Printing

- File › Export › Planting plan (PDF)… opens one side sheet beside a live preview: paper, what to include (plants, zones, notes, measurements, north arrow and scale), plant colours (As in the Design, Grayscale or Black), field sheets and page thumbnails.
- Add field sheet draws a detail page over any part of the Design; Split into readable sheets cuts a page that would print too small. Drag a page or use the arrow keys to move it.
- The plant key is grouped by habit (Tree, Shrub, Herbaceous, Climber, Other) with "(continued)" headings, and Find in key (Ctrl F) jumps to a species with its entry ringed.

### Saving, settings and start

- Canopi saves a moment after every change and whenever you switch away or close. A new Design is a Draft until you Save as… a file. You are asked only when a save fails or the file changed outside Canopi; Revert to the version when opened… undoes a session.
- The Start screen shows Recent Designs with a small sketch and counts ("2,201 plants · 24 zones"), Show in folder and Remove from list, and lists your Drafts. A file that moved, was made by another version or is damaged says so.
- Settings has Appearance, Map and imagery, New Designs (what a new Design starts with: satellite, symbol size, labels), Keyboard (turn single-key shortcuts off for dictation and screen readers), Files and data (where Drafts and the Data library live) and About.
- Menus, dialogs and panels fit small windows: long menus scroll, the tool and panel rails fold into More, and dialogs are modal. The map is a keyboard stop (F6 moves between title bar, tools, map and panel); screen readers announce tools and the map.

### Web and phones

- The Web Edition keeps Designs in the browser (Download a copy to keep a file), imports and exports GeoJSON, exports the PDF and Budget CSV, and presents stories.
- Below 640 px, the Web Edition switches to a phone layout: the map fills the screen under a slim bar with Menu, the Design name, Undo and place search; Select, Pan, Place plants and Polygon zone on a strip; panels in a sheet from the bottom (Layers, Plants, Catalog, More) that opens to half or the whole screen. Controls are at least 44 px and fields do not zoom the page.

### Languages

- Eleven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Counts, prices and dates follow your language's formats; the calendar week starts on its first day.

## Before you upgrade

- Designs from Canopi 1.2 and the 2.0 previews open and are upgraded as they load; a 1.2 Design without a location asks where your site is first. The file is left as it is until your first change, which saves it in the Canopi 2 format that 1.x cannot read: keep a copy if you still need 1.x. Designs from Canopi 1.1 and earlier are refused unchanged; open and save them once in 1.2 first.
- Stamps saved with earlier 2.0 previews can no longer be placed or exported: place the objects again and save the stamp anew.
- Terrain and height data imported in earlier previews is removed on first start and must be imported again.
- Web Edition data from 1.x is ignored.

## Known limits

- Importing terrain data and calculating Slope need tools that the installers do not yet bundle: GDAL for import, and the GeoLibre tool for Slope. Without them, import and new analysis runs say they are unavailable; saved results stay readable.
- On the Web, the PDF plant key is one ungrouped list, because the Web catalog does not carry plant habits yet.
- The phone layout has not been checked on a real phone yet; full screen in Present is not offered on macOS.
- Hydrology and canopy analyses, and exporting a story, are planned and not in this release.
- Performance with very dense Designs is not tuned yet.
