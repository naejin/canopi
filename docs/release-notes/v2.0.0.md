# Canopi 2.0.0

Canopi 2 makes the map the canvas. Every Design is drawn over a basemap, satellite imagery or your own terrain data, and every plant, zone, note and measurement is stored where it is on Earth. Pan, zoom and search move only the view, never your objects. Desktop and Web share the same canvas, panels, stories and PDF export; Desktop adds the full Plant catalog, the Data library and the Design notebook.

## What's new

### Designing on the map

- A new Design opens with "Where is your site?": search a place or type coordinates and the map flies there. Fit to Design frames what the title bar, rails and open panel leave visible.
- Backgrounds: OpenFreeMap map styles (Liberty, Positron, Bright, Dark) with labels in your language, satellite imagery (free, or your own Google Maps key kept on this device), or None. Contour lines and hillshading from online elevation are one switch away.
- Every tool shows a card beside the tool rail with what to do next and its keys; Select's card names its modifiers in one line. Place plants previews the plant under the pointer with its mature width and the distance to its nearest neighbour; Plant a row takes an interval; Place a stamp turns with [ and ].
- The floating selection toolbar is gone. Right-click an object (or press the Menu key) for one menu in plain words: Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate, Select all of this species, Plant color, Plant symbol, Show name, Arrange, Save as stamp, Lock, Rotate…, Species details, Add to calendar…, Set unit cost… and Rename zone….
- A chip at the bottom of the map names the selection ("12 plants · 3 species · 0.52 m apart", a zone's area and perimeter). Zones can be named; an unnamed one is called by shape and size ("Rectangle zone · 120 m²").
- Rotate by a typed angle or in 15° steps, nudge with the arrow keys (10 cm, or 1 m with Shift; with nothing selected they pan the map), lock and unlock objects, Unlock all in one step; Shift keeps polygon edges on 45° angles.
- Plant symbols were redrawn in one colour: 33 symbols in four families (plant form, what it gives, what it does, abstract marks), each taking any colour and legible at small sizes.
- Plants in this Design › Display on the map colours plants by species, stratum or one colour, sets symbol size and outline, and shows labels as codes or names. Soften background dims busy imagery.
- Text and labels stay readable in the dark theme and over satellite: their colour follows the map, with a halo.

### Finding and placing plants

- The Plant catalog opens on recommended species (edible and multi-use first) and sorts by Name, Height or Edibility. Quick filters sit under the search; other filters wait behind Filters and show as removable tokens.
- Species details lead with key facts: form, stratum, height, hardiness with USDA zones and lowest temperature, light, soil, moisture, growth and uses. Photos name their source and licence. A species already in your Design shows its code and count, with Select them and Zoom to them.
- Every plant list has the same finder (Ctrl F): common names in every language, scientific names and codes, ignoring accents, capitals and small typos. Selected on map, Stratum and Form narrow any list; matches are ringed on the map with Zoom to them and Select all.
- Place plants works before you pick a species: its card offers the species in the Design, your Favorites, recent picks, a search and the full catalog.
- A species with no name in your language shows its English name marked "(en)", in lists, details and the PDF.

### Layers, data and analyses

- Layers lists Design, one Site data row and Map (contour lines, hillshading, Background).
- Site data (Desktop) lists your terrain and height data, results under their source; rows show the value under the pointer, a click pins a point, colors and range are saved; Profile charts a line.
- Import terrain or height data… takes single-band GeoTIFFs (such as IGN LiDAR HD), says if they cover your site, and runs while you work.
- The Data library keeps imported data on this computer for all Designs. Remove from Design never deletes data; Delete everywhere does.
- Analyze… calculates Slope (degrees or percent) from ground elevation. Details show its source, whether it is out of date (with Refresh), its history and Run again with changes.

### Planning panels

- Calendar: Range, One day or Unscheduled in one control, a Completed switch, multi-line notes, and a week that starts on your language's first day. Add to calendar… creates an action for the selected plants or a zone.
- Budget: Set unit cost… from the right-click menu; Selected on map narrows the list; export as CSV from File › Export.
- Consortium assigns each species a stratum (Emergent, High, Mid, Low) and succession phases; the map's stratum colours and the Stratum filter follow it.

### Saved views and stories

- View › Save current view… keeps where the map looks, with its background, layers, labels and focused species, inside the Design. Saved views show a picture of each; going to one moves only the camera.
- The Stories panel builds a story from views: add the current view as a step, give it a title, text with bold, italic, bullets and links, and pictures (up to 1 MB each, 10 MB per Design). Drag steps or use Alt ↑ and Alt ↓; duplicate, move or delete them with Undo.
- Present shows a story full-window on desktop, web and phones: the step's text and pictures beside the live map, step dots, Previous and Next, keys, and swiping on a phone. Presenting never changes the Design.

### Printing

- File › Export › Planting plan (PDF)… opens one side sheet beside a live preview: paper, what to include, plant colours (As in the Design, Grayscale or Black), field sheets and page thumbnails.
- Add field sheet draws a detail page over any part of the Design; Split into readable sheets cuts a page that would print too small. Drag a page or use the arrow keys to move it.
- The plant key is grouped by habit (Tree, Shrub, Herbaceous, Climber, Other), and Find in key (Ctrl F) jumps to a species with its entry ringed.

### Saving, settings and start

- Canopi saves a moment after every change and whenever you switch away or close. A new Design is a Draft until you Save as… a file. You are asked only when a save fails or the file changed outside Canopi; Revert to the version when opened… undoes a session.
- The Start screen shows Recent Designs with a sketch and counts ("2,201 plants · 24 zones"), Show in folder and Remove from list, and lists your Drafts. A file that moved, was made by another version or is damaged says so.
- Settings has Appearance, Map and imagery, Canvas (whether the scroll wheel zooms or pans the map; pinch and Ctrl + wheel always zoom), New Designs (what a new Design starts with), Keyboard (turn single-key shortcuts off for dictation and screen readers), Files and data (where Drafts and the Data library live) and About.
- Help › Command palette… (Ctrl Shift P) runs any command by name. Menus, dialogs and panels fit small windows: long menus scroll, the rails fold into More, dialogs are modal. The map is a keyboard stop (F6 moves between title bar, tools, map and panel); screen readers announce tools and the map.

### Web and phones

- The Web Edition keeps Designs in the browser (Download a copy to keep a file), imports GeoJSON, exports GeoJSON, PDF and Budget CSV, and presents stories.
- On phones, the Web Edition switches to a phone layout: the map fills the screen under a slim bar (Menu, Design name, Undo, place search); Select, Pan, Place plants and Polygon zone on a strip; panels in a bottom sheet (Layers, Plants, Catalog, More) that opens to half or the whole screen. Controls are at least 44 px and fields do not zoom the page.

### Languages

- Eleven languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Counts, prices and dates follow your language's formats; the calendar week starts on its first day.

## Before you upgrade

Canopi 2.0 does not carry data over from earlier versions, and deletes none of it.

- Designs from Canopi 1.x and from the 2.0 previews say "Made with Canopi before 2.0; Canopi 2.0 and later can’t open it" and are left unchanged.
- From Canopi 1.x, Favorites, Recent Designs, the Design notebook, saved stamps, settings and Drafts move aside unchanged on first start (`user.db.before-2.0-…`, `drafts.before-2.0-…` in the data folder). Recent 2.0 previews keep all but older-format Drafts. On the Web they move to a `before-2.0` browser key, or, when the browser is full, stay hidden until you delete Drafts and reload. Canopi says so once. Data from a newer Canopi is refused and left untouched.
- Saved stamps from the earliest 2.0 previews stay in the list but can no longer be placed.
- If the Data library cannot be read as it is, Canopi rebuilds it from the files it keeps and says so; items marked failed come back with Retry. A library saved by a newer Canopi is left untouched and read-only.

## Known limits

- Importing terrain data needs no extra install: the Data library reads, converts and places rasters itself (no GDAL). The GeoLibre tool for Slope is included in the installers.
- On the Web, the PDF plant key is one ungrouped list, because the Web catalog does not carry plant habits yet.
- The phone layout (narrow, or short landscape windows) has not been checked on a real phone yet; full screen in Present is not offered on macOS.
- Hydrology and canopy analyses, and exporting a story, are planned and not in this release.
- Performance with very dense Designs is not tuned yet.

## Source code

Canopi is AGPL-3.0 free software; its installers include the GeoLibre tool, which contains the AGPL-3.0-or-later `wbspatialstats` tools. The complete source of this release is Canopi at tag `v2.0.0` plus the `geolibre-rust` and `whitebox-wasm` revisions in `desktop/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, linked from the release page.
