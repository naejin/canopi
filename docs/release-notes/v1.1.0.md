# Canopi 1.1.0 Release Notes

## Canvas PDF Export

- Create PDF overviews and detailed field sheets from your Design in Desktop and Web Edition.
- Draw Print Areas, choose included Layers, and adjust paper size, orientation, scale, and page zoom with a shared preview and export layout.
- Read complete Species legends, planting-strip labels, and shared-Species brackets on detailed sheets, with embedded multilingual fonts.

## Reading And Organizing Designs

- Inspect dense plantings with the Inspection Lens and zoom-dependent labels and annotation markers.
- Use the Species Key and short Species codes to identify and focus on plants in the Design.
- Access Canvas and Location tools and the Species Key, Layers, Design Notebook, Species Catalog, and Favorites through the unified right dock.
- Choose from the updated botanical and abstract Plant symbols with refined color and symbol pickers.

## Performance And Reliability

- Reduced repeated drawing, text-layout work, and offscreen Plant rendering in PixiJS and the Canvas2D fallback.
- Reused Plant-spacing calculations across unchanged Scene snapshots and reduced unnecessary Scene copying during pan, zoom, selection, and map/chrome updates.
- Preserved current live-edit geometry, defensive Scene ownership, and exact canvas/map camera synchronization while reducing background work.
