# Canvas workspace

Read the [design system](../system.md) first. Runtime ownership, gestures and rendering: [map workspace guide](../../docs/guides/map-workspace.md). Canvas boards: Workspace, SiteFound, Overview, NamesOnMap, PlacePlants, PlantRow, StampPlace, Selection, Appearance, ZoneDraw, ZoneSelected, MeasureText.

## Chrome

- Tool rail (left, floating): groups separated by rules; the active tool is solid ochre with `aria-pressed`. Arrow keys move between tools (roving tabindex). Labels and key hints show until each tool has been used once (per device), then the rail shrinks to 52 px icons with labelled tooltips on the right. The labelled rail is a fixed 224 px so it does not jump between tools. When the labelled rail would leave less than 360 px of map before the right chrome (a narrow window with a dock open), it keeps to icons with labelled tooltips until there is room again; it does the same when a name would be cut off in the 224 px rail (a long German label such as "Wiederherstellen" beside its keys), since a cut name is no name. View › Tool names stays the preference. When the window is too short for every tool above the view chip, the last tools fold, in rail order, into a More tools button (a menu with each tool, its key and a check on the active one) just before Undo and Redo, which never fold; More takes a soft accent while it holds the active tool, sits in the arrow-key order and opens with ArrowRight, Enter or Space. The rail never scrolls or covers the view chip. Plant colour and symbol are not on the rail; they open from the right-click menu.
- Panel rail (right, floating): panels as icon buttons with labelled tooltips, groups separated by a rule, the open panel solid ochre. The inspection launcher sits in its column above the zoom group. When the window is too short for every panel above them, the last panels fold, in rail order, into a More button (a menu with each panel, its shortcut and a check on the open one) at the rail's end; More takes a soft accent while it holds the open panel. The rail never scrolls or covers the launcher or the zoom group.
- View chip (bottom left): Grid, Snap to grid, Rulers as pressed toggles with a check icon; off by default except Snap.
- Zoom group (bottom right): scale bar, zoom out, scale ratio (a menu of common scales), zoom in, Fit to Design (Shift F). The attribution pill sits immediately left of the group, bottom-aligned, between the view chip and the group; it stays expanded (imagery terms require it) and folds into an (i) button only when that band leaves it less than 360 px, the room for one line (a narrow window, or a narrow visible map with a panel open), so it never wraps up under a panel. Below 0.1 px/m the Design is shown as one pin ("Return to …"), editing tools are disabled and a top-centre chip says "Zoom in to edit" with Return to Design.
- The map is always the background; there is no grid or ruler frame unless turned on. The map is a keyboard stop in the Tab order ("Design map", described by its keys: tool keys, Shift F10, arrows, F6, Esc), with the blue focus ring drawn inside its edge because it is full-bleed, and one of the four F6 areas (title bar, tool rail, map, open panel). Arrow keys on the map nudge the selection 10 cm, 1 m with Shift, as one undo per series; locked objects stay put. Rulers start right of the tool rail and below the title bar.
- The visible map area is what the title bar, rails, open dock and bottom chrome leave uncovered. Fit to Design, Zoom to them and Return to Design frame into it, and top-centre status chips centre in it, wrapping when it is narrow.

## Map annotations

- Measurements: light tag (95% surface, ink, 13/600, tabular). The live value under the cursor: dark tag. Zone names and text notes: ink that follows the map underneath, never the UI theme: cream text with a dark halo over satellite and the Dark basemap, dark text with a cream halo over the light basemap and paper; no pill. Lengths use one decimal below 100 m, none above, two below 1 m; areas in ha or m².
- Every overlay stroke (drawing lines, dashed rings, measurement lines) has a dark casing underneath so it reads on bright or dark imagery. Handles are 10 px with a 44 px touch hit area.
- Highlight rings (finder matches, a panel pointing at or selecting plants, including the map overlay a panel draws) are solid ochre, 2 px, over a 5 px halo, and never smaller than 8 px radius on screen, so they stay visible at the whole-Design zoom over basemap, satellite and paper. A panel never marks a plant with a filled disc or a colour of its own.
- Labels (View › Labels ▸ None, Codes, Names; N on the map cycles them): text with a contrasting halo (cream on dark backdrops, dark on light ones), below or above the symbol, thinned so no label touches another label or another plant's symbol. After a change a top chip says how many are shown for four seconds ("Codes shown for 70 of 282 plants in view"); Plants in this Design keeps the same line under its Labels choice. When none shows at this zoom, both add "Zoom in to see names" (or codes), a one-step zoom in. A saved view records the choice and its snapshot draws it. Pinned names and a single selection's name show whatever the choice.
- The plant hover card has a caret to its plant and a ring on the plant; it also appears on keyboard focus and dismisses with Esc.

## Selection and the right-click menu

- Selection box: 2 px ochre over a 5 px cream casing, square corner handles, and an ochre ring on each selected plant. Rotatable selections keep one rotate handle above; it is the only control drawn on a selection, and it stays inside the visible map area, so a selection near the tool rail or under the title bar keeps its handle reachable. There is no action bar.
- A status chip at the bottom centre of the visible map area, just above the view chip and zoom group (so it never meets them or the top finder chip), names the selection: the plant ("Apricot"; a species with no name in the interface language by its English name marked "(en)"), "12 plants · Apricot · 0.52 m apart", "12 plants · 3 species · 0.8 m apart" (the mean distance from each selected plant to its nearest selected neighbour), "Zone · Z04 · 118 m² · 46 m" (area and perimeter; a line zone gives its length), "Text note · Mulch in November" (its first line, cut at 40 characters), "Measurement · 12.4 m", or a mixed count ("4 selected · 2 plants · 1 zone · 1 text note"); groups count as their members. It never shows an id: a zone the user has not named is named by its type ("Rectangle zone · 120 m² · 44 m"). Lengths and areas follow the map annotations' rounding, through `Intl`. A measure never breaks inside; the chip keeps one line at 1280 px and wraps between its parts when narrow. Its text is a polite live region. It carries its actions: Select all of this species (one species, not yet all of it; Ctrl Shift A), Rename… (one unlocked zone; opens Rename zone) and Clear selection. It hides in overview and while "Where is your site?" shows.
- Right-click on an object selects it (unless it is already in the selection) and opens the context menu in plain words with the menu bar's shortcuts, under a quiet heading that names the selection in the selection chip's words ("2 plants · Apple · 1 m apart"; it is also the menu's accessible name), grouped by separators: Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate · on plants: Select all of this species, Plant color ▸, Plant symbol ▸, Show name (Hide name when every name shows), Species details (opens the species in the Plant catalog; one species only) · Add to calendar… (opens the Calendar's new action aimed at the selected plants, or at a lone zone; disabled otherwise), on plants Set unit cost… (opens the Budget with that species' unit cost focused, showing its row through any filters; one species only) · Arrange ▸ (Bring to front, Send to back · Group, Ungroup; disabled when none applies), Rotate… (Ctrl Alt R), Save as stamp (Desktop) · Lock, Unlock · Delete, last, in danger red. Arrange keeps the plant menu to 17 items, so it shows whole in a 1280 × 800 window. The Edit menu keeps Group and Ungroup beside its own Arrange ▸ (Bring to front, Send to back), as the Menus board shows. Zones get the same menu without the plant group and Set unit cost…; a lone zone's Add to calendar… targets that zone. A lone zone adds Rename zone… after Duplicate, in its own group (disabled while the zone is locked). Rename zone is a small modal (Name field, prefilled and selected; hint "Leave it empty to name the zone by its type and size."; Cancel, Rename): it changes only the display name, as one Undo. Right-click on the empty map offers Place plants here, then Paste (at the pointer) and Select all; the selection is kept. Place plants here arms Place plants and places the chosen species at the right-clicked point; with no species chosen, the tool card's chooser asks for one ("Choose a species first") and the pick lands at that point. Leaving Place plants or clicking the map first forgets the point.
- Items that do not apply stay listed and focusable with `aria-disabled`. Plant color ▸ and Plant symbol ▸ open the colour sheet and symbol picker beside the menu; Escape there returns focus to the map.
- The menu is as wide as its longest label and shortcut on one line (English, French, German), up to 400 px, and the heading stays above the items when they scroll. It opens at the pointer, flipping left or up to stay in view; when neither side has room it slides up beside the pointer until it ends inside the window, and scrolls only when it is taller than the window. Opened by the keyboard for a selection, it never slides over the selection: it takes the roomier side and scrolls. Arrange ▸ opens with ArrowRight, Enter or a click; Escape or ArrowLeft there returns to Arrange. The Menu key or Shift F10, while the map has focus, opens it for the selection just below its bounds (mid-map without a selection). Items are `menuitem`s; arrow keys, Home and End move; Enter runs; Escape and Tab close and return focus to the map; an outside press or scroll closes without moving focus. A locked or hidden hit shows the menu with everything but Paste disabled.

## Rotate…

A small modal dialog (Edit › Rotate…, the right-click menu, Ctrl Alt R) titled "Rotate selection": the Angle field in degrees (focused and selected, locale decimals, a minus sign or ° accepted) between −15° and +15° step buttons, a hint that it turns the selection about its center with positive angles clockwise, then Cancel and Rotate. Enter applies; a value that is not an angle keeps the dialog open with an alert under the field. The turn is one Undo. It is available for what the rotation handle turns: never a locked object, a measurement or a lone plant.

## Tools

- Tool cards (top left beside the rail, 320 px): the species glyph in its map colour (Place plants with a species, Plant a row once a plant is picked) or the stamp icon (Place a stamp) before the tool name; then the subject on its own line (the species or stamp, bold) and the instruction under it ("Pommier cultivé", "Click the map to place one"), so a long name never breaks the instruction; then quiet key hints ending with the Esc meaning ("Esc to stop placing" for placing tools, "Esc to cancel" while a gesture is in progress, otherwise "Esc to go back to Select"). Placing tools offer Change species / Change stamp: on Desktop, where stamps are saved, Place a stamp's link (Choose a saved stamp before anything is held) opens a chooser in the card with the saved stamps (stamp icon, name, "3 plants · 2 species") and "Copy an object on the map"; choosing gives the map focus back and Esc closes it. A held stamp's card ends its hints with "[ and ] rotate by 15°" and, once turned, says so in the instruction ("3 plants · 1 species · turned 45° · click to place"). The card hides for Select and Pan, in overview and while "Where is your site?" shows; its text is a polite live region, so a tool change is announced. The highlight chip starts right of it. Plant a row uses the same card: "Click a placed plant to repeat it along a row", then the plant's name · "drag along the row", the Interval field (focused when a plant is picked; Enter keeps it and returns to the map, Esc drops the plant) and the live count ("12 generated", ochre when dense, red with "Increase interval or shorten the line" above the limit), then "Shift keeps 45° angles · Esc to cancel".
- Place plants arms without a species: its card then carries a compact species chooser (the plant finder, with Stratum and Form, over the species in this Design, then Favorites, then recent picks, and an "Open the full catalog" link). Its rows are species rows: the glyph in the colour and symbol the species has, or would take, in this Design, then the names (an English name marked "(en)" when the interface language has none, here and in the card once chosen). Pointing at or focusing a row rings that species' plants with the hover stroke; nothing stays ringed while placing (an open species detail drops its rings while Place plants is armed). Choosing names the species in the card and gives the map focus back; Change species reopens the chooser and Esc closes it. A click on the map with no species places nothing: the card says "Choose a species first" and focuses the chooser.
- Place plants previews under the pointer: the species' symbol at 85% opacity, a dashed ring for its mature width labelled "Mature width up to 6 m" (the catalog's maximum width; no ring when the catalog has none, never an invented value) and a dashed line to the nearest plant within 320 px labelled "1.8 m to Apple". Plant a row shows spacing and the live plant count; previews use the real symbol size at 85% opacity. Place a stamp previews the whole stamp as ghosts under the pointer, whether copied from the map or saved; [ and ] rotate it by 15° about the pointer (anticlockwise, clockwise), and the objects it places keep the turn as a selection rotation would give it (plants move and stay upright, notes and zones turn). A new stamp starts upright. The brackets act only while a stamp is held (otherwise they stay Send to back and Bring to front) and, with single-key shortcuts off, only while the map has focus.
- Zones: click adds corners, first corner or Enter finishes, Backspace removes the last corner, Shift keeps 45°. Live edge lengths and area. A selected zone shows a sheet beside it: name, colour (radio swatches), fill, area and perimeter, Lock, Delete zone.
- Text note: click places a field; Enter finishes (never while an input method composes), Shift Enter breaks a line, Esc restores. Measure: click two points; the result stays as a guide.
- Drawing never moves existing objects; Space pans temporarily.
- Esc on the map cancels the gesture in progress, then returns to Select, then clears the selection. Closing a field the tool opened (a text note, the row spacing) returns focus to the map, so the chain continues.

## Symbol and colour

A non-modal sheet beside the selection: glyph and "Symbol and color" title with the species and selection counts; symbol tiles in three labelled groups (Plant form, What it gives, What it does) as one radio group, 5 columns, 70 px tiles with two-line labels; colour swatches with names as a radio group plus Custom…; footer "Only these N plants" (secondary) and "All N of this species" (primary). Choices preview until applied.

## Inspection lens

Unchanged in behaviour (see the map workspace guide): a view-only magnified preview with recentre, widen and magnify controls, collision-aware labels, keyboard pan, and an ochre source rectangle on the map. It uses the floating panel style and the shared icon-only rules.

## Presenting a story

Boards StoryPresent and StoryPhone. The map fills the window with nothing of the editing chrome (title bar, rails, dock, chips, grid, rulers, selection handles). A floating card (420 px, 32 px from the top left, glass, scrolls when long): "Story · Step n of m" in muted caption, the step title in Literata (28), the text at 18 px, its images with their descriptions, tags for the highlighted species with their plant counts ("Goji · 232 plants"), then Previous, step dots (buttons named "Step n: title", the current one a wide ochre bar with `aria-current="step"`) and Next (primary), and the hint "← → or Space to move · Esc to leave". Top right, a glass pill with the story name, Full screen (F) and Leave presentation (Esc). The map flies between steps (jumps with reduced motion) and rings what the step highlights. Keys: ← → PageUp PageDown, Space away from a button, Home, End, F, Esc; Tab stays inside the presenter, Previous and Next stay focusable at either end (`aria-disabled`), and each step is announced ("Step n of m: title"). Under 600 px: a bar on top with "Step n of m", small dots and Leave (44 px), and the card as a bottom sheet with full-width Previous and Next that stay in reach while the text scrolls, "Swipe left or right to move", safe-area insets.
